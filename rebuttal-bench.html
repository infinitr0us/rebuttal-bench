Compaction

Shorter source, the same rendering on OpenReview: every change is checked against the rendered comment and dropped if anything would differ (the last group excepted, and off until you switch it on). Copy compact copies the short form; the editor keeps your text. Notification e-mails show the Markdown rendered but formulas as LaTeX source, so only the formula tricks change what a reviewer reads there.

Wording

Shorter ways to say the same, for a draft over the limit. Advice only: nothing here changes your text, and some phrases are worth their length. Click a phrase to go to it, and again for its next use.

Sections

The length of each part of this draft, counted like the limit. A heading starts a part, and so does a paragraph, list item or quote that opens with a label such as **W1.**, **Q2:** or W3:. Click a part to go to it.

Promises

Select a sentence to open it in its draft. Copy future changes as a revision checklist.

Anonymity

Words that would name you, one per line: your names, e-mail addresses, institution, lab, project and GitHub names. Each one found in a draft gets a card. Prefer full names: a surname alone also matches other people’s citations. Kept in this browser only, never in a draft, an export or a copy.

This browser cannot store the list, so it lasts until you leave the page.

History

Earlier versions are kept before Fix all, Apply to source, Replace all, Clear, Load example and a restore, and every 10 minutes while you edit: up to 20 per draft, in this browser only. When they take up too much room, or a draft needs the space, the oldest go first.

Drafts are kept in this browser only, and the online page and a downloaded copy of it each keep their own. Export them to keep a backup, or to copy them into another browser. Import adds the drafts of an exported file as new tabs, and never replaces or changes a draft.
